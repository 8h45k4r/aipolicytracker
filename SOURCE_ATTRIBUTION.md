# Source Attribution Policy

AI Policy Tracker is *source-backed*: every policy, strategy, status, or regulatory fact in the tracker must be traceable to an official or openly licensed source. This policy explains what may be contributed and how to attribute it.

## What may be contributed

- **Metadata** about a policy: name, jurisdiction, governing body, announcement date, status, governance structure, partners, motivation, and a **link** to the official document.
- **Your own short summary** (a few sentences, in your own words) of what the document does.
- Facts taken from **official sources**: government portals, official gazettes and journals, legislatures, regulators, courts, and intergovernmental bodies (OECD, UN, EU institutions, etc.).
- Content under an **open licence** that permits reuse with attribution (for example public domain, CC0, CC BY 4.0, Open Government Licence). Record the licence with the contribution.

## What may not be contributed

- **Copyrighted legal commentary or analysis** from law firms, consultancies, publishers, or paid policy databases.
- **Standards text**, including ISO/IEC, IEEE, NIST-derived commercial reproductions, or any standard sold under licence. Link to the standard's official page instead.
- Long verbatim excerpts of any copyrighted document, even when the source is official, unless the source's licence explicitly permits it.
- Leaked, unpublished, or embargoed documents.
- Personal data of individuals (names, e-mail addresses, phone numbers) beyond a public official's title where relevant.

## How to attribute

For every contributed fact:

1. Put the canonical URL of the official document in the entry's `whitepaper_document_link` (or the news item's description).
2. In the pull request or issue, list each changed field with its source URL and the date accessed.
3. State the verification status: verified against the primary source, partially verified, or unverified.
4. If the source is openly licensed rather than official, name the licence.

## Removal requests

If you believe content in this project infringes your rights or reproduces licensed material, open an issue or contact the maintainers (see `SECURITY.md` for private contact) and it will be reviewed promptly.

## Disclaimer

Entries reflect the maintainers' and contributors' reading of public sources at the time of contribution. They are informational only and are not legal advice.

AI Incident Database report metadata (`data/external/aiid_reports.json`): titles, URLs, source domains, dates and author names of the news reports AIID catalogues, under CC BY-SA 4.0. Report texts and AIID descriptions are not reproduced; readers are sent to the original publisher.
