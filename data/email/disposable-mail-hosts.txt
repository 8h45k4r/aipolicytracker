# Mail exchangers operated by throwaway-inbox services.
#
# This is the part that actually works. A throwaway service hands out thousands
# of rotating domains, but it runs its own mail servers, so every one of those
# domains points at a handful of exchangers. Blocking the exchanger stops the
# domains nobody has catalogued yet — including the ones registered tomorrow.
#
# Derived by resolving MX records for a random sample of 400 known throwaway
# domains and keeping only the exchangers that no legitimate sender uses.
# Shared infrastructure that also served those domains was deliberately left
# out and must stay out, because real organisations use it: Cloudflare Email
# Routing (cloudflare.net), Google Workspace (google.com, googlemail.com),
# Microsoft 365 (outlook.com), Amazon SES (amazonaws.com), Mailgun
# (mailgun.org), ImprovMX (improvmx.com), Forward Email (forwardemail.net),
# Zoho (zoho.com) and shared hosting resellers.
#
# Matching is by suffix on the exchanger's hostname, so "mailinator.com" covers
# "mail2.mailinator.com". A false positive here is reported with
# `php artisan email:check <address>`, which names the exact line that decided.
