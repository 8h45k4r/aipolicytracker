## Fields

- **System ID** — Stable identifier (e.g. AIS-001).
- **System name** — Product or internal name.
- **Business owner** — Accountable person or team.
- **Technical owner** — Team that builds or operates it.
- **Purpose and use case** — What decision or output it produces and for whom.
- **Our role** — Provider, deployer, importer, distributor or downstream integrator (EU AI Act terms).
- **Model / vendor** — Underlying model(s) and supplier, including general-purpose models.
- **Data used** — Personal data, special categories, training vs inference data.
- **Affected people** — Customers, employees, applicants, public.
- **Markets** — Jurisdictions where it is placed on the market or used.
- **EU AI Act risk category** — Prohibited, high-risk (Annex I/III), transparency, minimal, or not assessed.
- **Other applicable rules** — Sector rules, state laws, data-protection duties.
- **Human oversight** — How a person can intervene or override.
- **Status** — Idea, pilot, production, retired.
- **Last review date** — Date of the last governance review.
- **Next review date** — Planned review.
- **Evidence links** — Where documentation, tests and approvals live.
