## Fields

- **Step** — Context, stakeholders, rights, likelihood/severity, mitigation, residual risk, consultation, decision.
- **Prompt** — Question to answer.
- **Our assessment** — Free text.
- **Evidence** — Supporting document or data.
- **Owner** — Person responsible.
- **Date** — When completed.
