## Fields

- **Jurisdiction** — Country, state or bloc (use the site slug for traceability).
- **Instrument** — Law, regulation, strategy or guidance name.
- **Status** — Proposed, adopted, in force, guidance.
- **Binding** — Yes / no.
- **Our role** — Provider, deployer, importer, distributor, GPAI provider.
- **Applies from** — Date the relevant obligations apply.
- **Supervisor** — Competent authority or regulator.
- **Key obligations** — Short list with article references.
- **Evidence expected** — Documentation, testing, registration, notices.
- **Owner** — Accountable person.
- **Source URL** — Official source (from the record page).
- **Last checked** — Date you last confirmed the row.
