# AI Vendor Due Diligence Questionnaire

_AI Vendor Due Diligence Questionnaire — AIPolicyTracker free template_

## Fields

- **Section** — Identity, purpose, data, testing, oversight, security, incidents, contract.
- **Question** — What to ask the vendor.
- **Why it matters** — Obligation or risk behind the question.
- **Evidence to request** — Document or artefact.
- **Vendor answer** — Free text.
- **Rating** — Acceptable, conditional, unacceptable.
- **Follow-up owner** — Person responsible.
