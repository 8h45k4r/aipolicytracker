## Fields

- **nist-ai-rmf** — iso-42001
- **Week** — 1 to 4, plus a day-30 review.
- **Goal** — Outcome for the week.
- **Tasks** — Concrete steps with the template to use.
- **Output** — The artefact produced.
- **NIST AI RMF function** — Govern, Map, Measure or Manage.

## Table

| nist-ai-rmf | Week | Goal | Tasks | Output | NIST AI RMF function |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Week 1 | Know what you have | Appoint the owner; fill the AI System Inventory Template; decide your role per system; list applicable laws per market | Inventory v1 | Govern, Map |
| Week 2 | Know what could go wrong | Fill the AI Risk Register Template for production systems; screen prohibited practices; classify EU AI Act risk | Risk register v1; screening record | Map, Measure |

| Day 30 | Review | Leadership review: risks accepted, budget for gaps, owners confirmed, next review date set | Signed review note | Govern |
