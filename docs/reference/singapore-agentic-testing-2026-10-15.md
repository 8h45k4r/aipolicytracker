# Singapore: agentic AI testing starter kit, research note, 15 October 2026

## What launched

On 14 October 2026, at the AI Assurance Exchange (agentic AI edition) in Singapore, IMDA released the **Starter Kit for Testing Agentic Systems for Safety and Reliability**. This title is as press coverage reports it; IMDA's own page and the kit document were not found. It was one of three announcements that day:

1. **The starter kit.** It sets out four steps:
   1. identify risks such as erroneous or unauthorised actions;
   2. design and run realistic tests that cause no real-world harm;
   3. analyse results and operational logs;
   4. monitor and retest continuously.

   It tests an agent's whole trajectory, its reasoning and actions, not only its final output. That means checking the tools it uses, the data it reaches, its decisions, whether it respects permissions, and whether safeguards hold. Suggested metrics include tool-use accuracy, handoff accuracy and human-intervention rates.

   It is reported to draw on consultations with more than 20 deployers and testers (Google, AWS and Resaro named), the Global AI Assurance Sandbox and the Singapore AI Safety Institute. Case studies come from Cohere, CyberSierra, Earlybird AI, KBTG and Knovel Engineering.
2. **The AI Tester Accreditation Programme (AI TAP)**, run by the AI Verify Foundation and announced in May 2026, named its first accredited testers: AIDX Tech, Knovel Engineering, PwC Singapore, Resaro and Vulcan. Accreditation lasts 12 months and does not certify that a tester is independent or that a tested system is safe.
3. **A Frontier AI Resilience and Security Taskforce (FAIRS)** for AI-enabled cyber threats to the infocomm and media sector. This is reported in the press only and is not recorded.

The kit extends the **Model AI Governance Framework for Agentic AI** (IMDA, 22 January 2026). That framework announced testing guidelines for agentic applications built on the **Starter Kit for Testing LLM-Based Applications for Safety and Reliability**: voluntary guidelines, v1.0 published January 2026 after a 2025 consultation.

## Anthropic

**No source on the kit names Anthropic.** On the same day, Anthropic announced a Singapore office and signed a memorandum of understanding with the Ministry of Digital Development and Information. Its scope:

- cooperation on AI safety, security and model evaluation;
- research on AI adoption;
- technical exchanges with Singapore's AI safety and assurance institutions on model evaluation, testing standards and incident response.

Anthropic took part in IMDA's Generative AI Evaluation Sandbox in 2023 and later in multilingual red-teaming with IMDA and the AI Verify Foundation (source: anthropic.com/news/singapore-office-opening). The MOU and the kit are separate announcements. The site should not describe Anthropic as a partner in the kit unless the kit document says so.
