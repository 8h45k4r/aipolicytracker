# Research round, 26 November 2026: sector rules, US states and international instruments

Prompted by a list of about 200 AI policies that a reader tracks. About 60 were already on record. This round
added 96 instruments and five US state jurisdictions (Arizona, Indiana, New Jersey, North Dakota,
Pennsylvania), all `pending_review`.

## How the facts were gathered (read before reviewing)

The research environment could not open official pages: legislature, regulator, gazette and government hosts
were refused by its network policy. Every fact comes from search-engine extracts of the official URLs
recorded on each instrument, as in the [15 October round](subnational-research-2026-10-15.md). So:

- every new record and jurisdiction is `pending_review`, confidence `medium` at most (`low` where the
  extracts were thin, conflicting or only a press release);
- a date that was not confirmed is left null and explained in `date_notes`; a date the reader's list gave
  but no official extract confirmed was not used;
- nothing rests on secondary sources alone; where enactment was shown only by secondary reporting the record
  was held back (below);
- summaries are written fresh from the official extracts. The reader's list came from a commercial
  database and was used only as a list of names to look for.

General laws that also govern AI (GDPR, UK GDPR, APPI, FCRA, ECOA, 42 CFR Part 2, NIST SP 800-53 and others)
are recorded with `ai_specific: false`.
