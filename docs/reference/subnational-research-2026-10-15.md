# Sub-national AI laws and policies: research round, 15 October 2026

Prompted by Secure AI Project's note that state-level developments were hard to find, with the three US frontier-AI transparency laws named: California SB 53, the New York RAISE Act and Illinois SB 315.

## What changed on the site

- **Country pages list their states' laws in the body.** Until now a country's sub-national records were only named in the sidebar, so SB 53 and the RAISE Act (both on record and verified since September) were easy to miss from the United States page. The table is headed "State AI laws and policies" (or "State, provincial and local …" where the children differ).
- **37 new sub-national jurisdictions and 53 new records**, all `pending_review`, plus four change events.

## How the facts were gathered (read before reviewing)

The research environment could not open the official pages: legislature and government hosts were refused by its network policy. Every fact comes from search-engine extracts of the official URLs recorded on each instrument, which can be wrong. So:

- every new record and jurisdiction is `pending_review`, with confidence `medium` at most (`low` where the source is a press release or the number is in doubt);
- a date that was not confirmed is left null and explained in `date_notes`;
- nothing was added from secondary sources alone; items whose only source was a news outlet are listed below as leads.

A reviewer should open each official URL, confirm the title, number, dates and duties, and then verify the record in the admin.

On the "three frontier transparency laws": the extracts support Illinois SB 315 as a frontier-AI safety and transparency law modelled on SB 53, adding annual third-party audits. Connecticut's PA 26-15 also covers large frontier developers, but through whistleblower and internal-reporting duties rather than a published framework. The New York RAISE Act was rewritten by a chapter amendment (S8828 / A9449, chapter 96, signed 27 March 2026), recorded as a change event; the RAISE record itself is unchanged until a reviewer reads the amended text.

## Reviewer checklist

1. Open the official URL on each new record; confirm the title, number, adoption and effective dates, and the summary.
2. Illinois SB 315: confirm the effective date in the enrolled Act and the penalty provisions.
3. New York RAISE: read the chapter amendment and update the RAISE record (scope, dates, oversight office).
4. California SB 942: confirm its own chapter number and how AB 853 changed the operative dates.
5. Tuscany, Montana, Odisha, Maharashtra, Seoul, Dubai Blueprint: confirm numbers and dates (all recorded at `low` confidence).
6. Then verify each record in the admin review queue; jurisdictions can be verified once their instruments are.
