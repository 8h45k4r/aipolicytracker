# Research and improvements, 1 October 2026

Three questions were investigated: where the site falls short for search and answer engines (Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT search, Perplexity, Copilot); what user management and role permissions the admin lacks; and which AI governance templates teams still need that the library does not have. This note records what was found, what was changed in this round, and what is left, ranked.

## 1. Search and answer engines

### What was already strong

A per-page SEO object with fitted titles, canonical, robots and Open Graph; JSON-LD for Organization, WebSite with SearchAction, Legislation, Dataset, FAQPage, HowTo, NewsArticle and BreadcrumbList; answer boxes and key facts on policies, obligations and jurisdictions; a sitemap index with a news sitemap; `llms.txt`, `llms-full.txt`, Markdown context files and open data; AI crawlers explicitly allowed; `seo:audit` crawling every sitemap URL in CI.

### What was wrong, and what was done

| # | Finding | Why it mattered | Done |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Every deploy moved every record's `updated_at` to the deploy time: the importer set `published_at = now()` on each run, rebuilt sections with new ids, and stamped sub-national jurisdictions through an Eloquent `update()`. All 187 policies shared one modified date. | `dateModified`, sitemap `lastmod` and the social-card version all said "changed today" for every page. Search engines learn to ignore a freshness signal that is always fresh. | Import is now idempotent: first publication is kept, sections are updated in place, the parent link is written only when it differs. Re-importing unchanged data changes 0 of ~750 rows (test: `SeoFreshnessTest`). Verified records also carry `lastReviewed`. |
| 2 | No IndexNow. | Bing's index feeds Copilot and ChatGPT search; corrections reached them on the next crawl, not the next deploy. | `IndexNow` service, `/{key}.txt` key file, submission of only the records whose content changed after each import, and `php artisan seo:indexnow`. Off until `INDEXNOW_KEY` is set. |
| 3 | No glossary. The defined vocabulary (actors, system types, risk categories, evidence types) was never published as terms. | Definitional questions ("what is a deployer under the EU AI Act") are the commonest AI-governance queries, and answer engines quote short definitions with stable anchors. | `/glossary`: 22 sourced explanations plus the defined vocabulary, one anchor per term (`/glossary#deployer`), a `DefinedTermSet`, links to the duties, controls and templates for each term, listed in the sitemap, `llms.txt` and the footer. |
| 5 | The three curated comparisons (EU vs UK, EU vs US, Singapore vs Australia) had no FAQ; generated pairs did. | The highest-intent comparison pages had no question-and-answer content at all. | Curated pages fall back to the computed questions. |
| 6 | Answer boxes on three page types only. | Control and change pages opened with a heading and prose, not an answer. | Composed answers on control pages (owner, cadence, duties served, evidence) and change pages (what, where, when, status afterwards, in practice), with key facts on changes. |
| 7 | `llms.txt` listed only the templates index; no individual templates, no curated comparisons. | Assistants following `llms.txt` could not reach those pages. | Every template and curated comparison is listed. |
| 8 | Transition measure pages were in no sitemap. | Indexable pages reachable only by links. | Added, on the same indexability rule the page applies to itself. |
| 9 | Organization had no editorial-policy properties. | Answer engines weigh a source's stated publishing, correction and feedback policies. | `publishingPrinciples`, `correctionsPolicy`, `actionableFeedbackPolicy`, `ownershipFundingInfo`. |
| 10 | Template pages claimed open public read access while the files sit behind a work-email form. | Markup that promises what the page does not deliver. | `conditionsOfAccess` and a free `Offer` instead. |
| – | The named AI-crawler group in `robots.txt` overrode `*` and so dropped five disallow rules. | Assistant crawlers could fetch download and unsubscribe endpoints. | The groups are identical, with a test that keeps them so. |

### Left, ranked

1. **Pages competing for the same query.** `/ai-policy-nepal` vs `/ai-regulation-nepal`; `/eu-ai-act` vs `/policies/eu-ai-act`; `/ai-regulation-{india,uk,usa,australia}` vs `/jurisdictions/{slug}`. Two pages splitting links means answer engines often cite neither. Recommendation: make the country hubs the single page for each country (fold the landing copy in, then 301 the landings), and point `/eu-ai-act` at the policy page. This is an editorial decision about which copy survives, so it is left to the maintainer.
2. **Bylines on editorial pages.** Guides, landings and comparisons name the Organization as author and show no visible date. Add the maintainer as `author` (Person) with a visible "Updated" line.
3. **Dataset scope.** Dataset markup appears on home, calendar and every record page, which dilutes Dataset Search; keep it on `/open-data` and the per-record pages, and add the CSV and NDJSON distributions, `temporalCoverage` and `spatialCoverage` there.
4. **More `seo:audit` checks.** Duplicate descriptions, JSON-LD that does not parse, FAQ markup without a visible FAQ, hreflang reciprocity.
5. **Set `INDEXNOW_KEY` in production** (`openssl rand -hex 16`); nothing is submitted until it is.

## 2. User management and role permissions

### Before

Three fixed roles (Editor, Reviewer, Analyst) with capabilities in code; owners from `ADMIN_EMAILS`; per-row role change, suspend, restore, second-factor reset and delete; search and two filters.

### Done

- **Editable role permissions.** Owners edit a role × capability matrix (`/backend/admin/users/permissions`), with a marker on every cell that differs from the default, a reset per role, and password confirmation to save. Settings, billing and user management stay owner-only: refused by validation, and ignored if written straight to the table, so the matrix cannot become a route to ownership. Defaults live in code; a role with no edit uses them.
- **A page per account.** What it can do (capability by capability, and where that comes from), sign-in and invitation history, the role grant, the changes made to it and the changes it made, and every action in one place.
- **Invitations.** Create an account with a role and a personal note; a week-long link to choose a password, which also verifies the address. Owner addresses and existing accounts are refused.
- **Bulk actions.** Set role, suspend (with reason), restore, re-send verification, and delete (behind password confirmation). Your own account and owners are always skipped and counted.
- **Access review.** Last sign-in recorded; filters for admins without a second factor, admins not signed in for 90 days, and pending invitations; summary cards that open those lists; sorting; CSV export with formula-injection protection.

### Next

1. A quarterly access-review email to owners listing dormant admins and admins without a second factor.
2. Per-account session list with "sign out this device".
3. Bulk actions recorded per account in the audit log (today one row per bulk request).
4. SSO (SAML or OIDC) for teams, mapping groups to roles.

## 3. Templates

The library had 26 templates. Ten were added, chosen for demand from compliance, legal, risk and product teams and for having records to draw on:

| Template | Draws on |
|---|---|
| EU AI Act High-Risk Deployer Compliance Pack | 20 EU AI Act deployer duties, EU deadlines |
| AI Use-Case Intake and Triage Form | 6 prohibited practices on record, risk and impact duties |
| General-Purpose AI Model Provider Compliance Kit | 16 GPAI provider duties |
| EU AI Act Conformity Assessment and QMS Workbook | 16 EU requirements across nine areas |
| AI Supplement to a DPIA | privacy and impact-assessment duties |
| AI Decision Explanation and Appeal Kit | 11 duties linked to the explanation-and-appeal control, across jurisdictions |
| Employment AI Bias Audit Kit | NYC Local Law 144, Illinois HB 3773, Colorado SB 26-189 |
| AI Change Log and Substantial Modification Test | duties linked to the release and change-management control |
| Frontier AI Safety Framework and Transparency Report | duties linked to the frontier-model safety control (California SB 53 and others) |
| AI Regulatory Horizon Scan | the change log (41 changes in 12 months) and every upcoming deadline; the first template built on change events |

Every duty row links to its record and is rebuilt when the record changes. Next candidates: a public-sector AI use-case inventory (OMB M-25-21 and the procurement rules on record) and a synthetic-content provenance plan (EU Article 50, China's labelling measures, Korea).

## 4. The data itself

Search and templates are only as good as the records. The biggest single improvement left is verification: 13 records added from secondary sources in September (the EU Omnibus amendments, Colorado SB 26-189, the US transition bills, four California changes) are pending review and are labelled as such everywhere. Verifying them against the official sources, in the review queue, is what turns them into citable answers.
