each on its own branch, each with its own tests, each verified by the full
suite before it was pushed. This page records the final quality pass and the
manual steps that only the maintainer can do.

## Final QA

All figures below were produced in the build container on 26 September 2026
against the `seo/p10-authority` head, on a fresh import of `data/`.

**Test suite.** 416 tests, 30,713 assertions, green on SQLite in memory
(`php artisan test`). Pint, ESLint and the MCP server tests (`npm test`,
7 tests) pass. CI runs the same suite on SQLite and PostgreSQL.

**Crawl of every sitemap URL** (`php artisan seo:audit --fail-on-violation`,
the same check CI runs): 4,136 pages across twelve sections; 0 violations
after one fix. The crawl found 28 pages returning 500: the 14 change events a
template rebuild records (reviewed by `templates:build`, which is not a
person) broke the new reviewer lookup on the change page and in the news
sitemap; fixed, re-crawled, clean. Checks applied per page: status, title
present and ≤60 characters, no identifier leak, unique title, meta
description ≤155, canonical, single H1, valid JSON-LD, no noindex page in a
sitemap.

**OpenAPI.** `redocly lint` on the served document: 0 errors after declaring
the root `security: []` (the public operations) beside the bearer scheme the
watches endpoints use; 17 warnings remain, all "operation has no 4XX
response" on list endpoints, which is accurate. Every new entity is in the
document and covered by a two-way contract test (`tests/Support/
OpenApiContract.php`): templates, applicable deadlines, the register,
watches, transition measures, indicators, the index.

**MCP, llms.txt, exports.** Thirteen tools, every new entity represented:
`list_templates`, `get_applicable_deadlines`, `build_obligations_register`,
`list_transition_measures`, `get_displacement_index` added in this cycle.
`llms.txt` lists every new surface; bulk exports: the templates feed,
`/deadlines/which-date-applies.ics|.pdf`, the register in four formats, the
state-of CSV, the transition datasets as JSON.

130 KB) are the next cut.



## What only you can do

1. **Merge and deploy.** Merge `seo/p10-authority` into `main` (it contains
   P1–P9). On the server: `git fetch origin && git reset --hard origin/main`
   in `/opt/aip/repo` (the local history rewrite from earlier is not on the
   server yet), then run `deploy/deploy.sh`, which migrates, imports, and
   builds the templates. Two new dependencies install with Composer:
   `phpoffice/phpspreadsheet`, `phpoffice/phpword`, `dompdf/dompdf`.
2. **Branch protection on `main`** is still off since the history rewrite:
   re-add a classic rule requiring "PHP lint & tests", "JS lint & build" and
   "Gate check".
3. **Search Console.** Resubmit `/sitemap.xml`; it now carries the
   `updates`, `templates` and hub sections, and `/sitemap-news.xml` when a
   change is under 48 hours old. Ask for removal of the 104 sensitive incident
   URLs that `scripts/seo/sensitive-indexed` lists from a pages export.
4. **Google Publisher Center.** Register the site with `/sitemap-news.xml`
   as the news sitemap and `/changes/feed` as the feed.
5. **Reviewer assignments.** The five transition measures are drafts with
   every factual field empty: assign a reviewer to read the official texts
   (AI dividend and sovereign-wealth-fund proposals, the AI excise bill, the
   New York WARN amendment, the UBI proposals). Same for the EU Digital
   Omnibus dates and Colorado SB 26-189, which the Colorado kit and the EU
   classifier flag as not yet recorded. Six country hubs (Ghana, Sri Lanka,
   Pakistan, South Korea, Nigeria, Kenya) need one more sourced instrument
   each to be indexed. Three translated hubs need a reviewer to sign them
   before they are indexed.
6. **Weekly change logging.** The updates hub, the news sitemap, the digest
   and the watches all feed from `data/changes`; the last entry before this
   work was 14 July 2026. One dated, sourced entry a week keeps every
   surface alive.
7. **Journalist outreach for the index.** `/state-of-ai-regulation` and
   `/ai-economic-transition/methodology` are the pages to point at: the
   figures are computed, the method is published and versioned, and every
   count links to the records behind it.

## Follow-up, later on 26 September

A production 500 and an outside audit of the live site arrived after the
merge above. What changed, all on `main`:

**The 500.** Every `/reviewers/{slug}` page failed on PostgreSQL: the list of a
reviewer's confirmed records asked the `obligations` table for `reviewed_by`, a
column it does not have. SQLite reads an unknown double-quoted identifier as a
string, so the query passed every local test and the crawl. Fixed; a test now
listens to the page's queries. Every request also carries an id that appears on
the error page, in the log line and in the `X-Request-Id` header, and nginx
passes its own `$request_id` (`deploy/nginx-aip.conf`, redeploy to apply).

**Admin.** Reviewed end to end (`docs/reference/admin-review-2026-09-26.md`).
The review queue works on selections for every record kind; the inline
confirmations that the Content-Security-Policy had silently blocked now run.

**Data recorded from the audit's findings**, every one pending review because the
official texts could not be opened from the build container (EUR-Lex,
leg.colorado.gov and Congress.gov are blocked there):

- EU AI Act: Regulation (EU) 2026/1744 dates (2 December 2027 for Annex III,
  2 August 2028 for Annex I, two new prohibitions from 2 December 2026); the 34
  Chapter III obligations and the deadlines table follow; record moved from
  `needs_update` to `pending_review`.
- Colorado: SB 24-205 marked repealed (it never took effect); new record for
  SB 26-189 with five duties from 1 January 2027; templates cite the new law.
- Transition tracker: H.R. 10044 and S. 4825 recorded with sponsors and dates.
- Five change log entries (14 May, 18 June, 27 July, 2 August, 6 August 2026),
  so the updates hub and the news sitemap have Q3 entries again.

**What only a person can do now**, in this order:

1. Deploy `main` (the 500 fix and everything above are not live until then).
2. Open the four official texts and verify the records under your own name
   from `/backend/review` (tick, attest, save): EU AI Act and its obligations,
   both Colorado records, the two transition measures, the five change entries.
   Then `php artisan policy:export-verifications` and open the pull request.
   Your reviewer page shows 0 until you do; the editorial desk signed the rest.
3. Verify the three remaining transition drafts (or leave them drafts); the
   displacement index stays at zero until a named reviewer confirms measures.
4. Check the CI run on `main` for a red PostgreSQL job before the next deploy:
   it is the only automatic signal for the class of bug behind the 500.

Audit items that were already on `main` and only awaiting deploy: incident id
URLs 301 to their slugs (P1), sensitive incidents noindex with neutral titles
(P4), the visible register export and server-side watches (P8), localised hubs
